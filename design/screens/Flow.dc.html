Backend — what happens on Save
On Save, the server:
1. Checks GSTIN format, totals, e-way expiry
2. Finds party by GSTIN — else creates it
3. Finds truck by number — else creates it
4. Takes next LR no. inside one transaction
5. Stores LR + goods + charges, status Booked
6. Links LR ↔ tax invoice ↔ freight bill by ID
7. Remembers defaults for this party & route
8. Makes the LR PDF, ready to print / WhatsApp
